The Silent Standup

A Psychological Safety Simulation

You're a team lead running a remote sprint. Your four teammates each carry a hidden worry — a mistake, a confusion, a disagreement, an overload. They'll only speak up if you make it safe enough.

How to play: Read your team's chat messages, then choose how to respond. Your words shape whether people feel safe to take interpersonal risks — asking questions, admitting errors, challenging plans, or asking for help.

Win condition: Get at least 3 of 4 teammates to disclose their hidden concerns before the Friday deadline.
Lose condition: Fewer than 3 disclosures — hidden issues compound into project failure.

Mechanics based on Amy C. Edmondson's research on psychological safety (1999, 2002)
