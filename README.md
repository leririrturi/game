# The Silent Standup

**A Leadership Simulation on Psychological Safety and Interpersonal Risk-Taking**

Based on the research of **Amy C. Edmondson**:
- *Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams* (Administrative Science Quarterly, 1999)
- *Managing the Risk of Learning: Psychological Safety in Work Teams* (Blackwell Handbook of Organizational Teamwork, 2002)

---

## 🎯 Premise

You lead a remote team through a high-stakes 5-day sprint to launch an analytics dashboard. Each teammate harbors a critical, hidden concern:
- **Priya (Backend Engineer)**: Risk of *Being Seen as Incompetent* (database migration corrupted test data).
- **Marcus (UX Designer)**: Risk of *Being Seen as Ignorant* (requirements are unclear; designing based on guesswork).
- **Lia (QA Lead)**: Risk of *Being Seen as Negative* (architecture won't scale and risks production outages).
- **Tom (Frontend Engineer)**: Risk of *Being Seen as Disruptive* (triple-booked across projects, working until 2 AM).

Teammates perform a **tacit calculus** at every interaction, weighing the immediate social risk of speaking up against the long-term cost to the project. Only leader behaviors that foster psychological safety (fallibility, curiosity, non-defensiveness, framing challenges as shared learning) will unlock disclosure.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
2. Read the team's updates during the daily standups.
3. Choose your responses to ask questions, react to news, and frame sprint objectives.
4. Keep track of individual and team safety meters.
5. Win by surfacing at least **3 out of 4** hidden issues before Friday's deadline.
